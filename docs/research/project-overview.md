## Purpose and product scope

**Verified:** The app is a local Streamlit dashboard that accepts at most one checking CSV and one credit-card CSV. It normalizes transactions, summarizes cash flow, identifies recurring debits/credits, estimates card interest trends, and suggests matching payments across the two accounts. Users can inspect and export tables and save confirmation/rejection feedback. This matches the current README and v1.1.0 requirements, although package metadata still describes a checking-only app at version `0.1.1`. ## Implemented workflows

### Import and identity

**Verified:** Filenames must follow `<bank>__<account_type>__<account_number>.csv`; account types are `checking` and `creditcard`, and bank/account segments permit letters, digits, and hyphens. Each upload slot checks the expected type. A SHA-256 digest of the entire CSV byte sequence becomes the descriptor's fingerprint. The persistence key includes bank, type, account number, and this fingerprint. [filename rules](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/config.py:6), [loading](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/ingest/csv_loader.py:64), [storage key](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/domain/models.py:36)

The parser attempts UTF-8 with BOM, UTF-8, then CP1252. It recognizes case-insensitive Date/Amount/Description headers and optional Status, or positional three-column and five-plus-column layouts. Dates accept month/day/year or ISO dates; amounts become cent-quantized Decimals. Raw and normalized descriptions, original row number, and optional status are retained; nonnegative values are credits. Normalization uppercases and strips configured bank/card/reference noise and nonletters. [decoding and parsing](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/ingest/csv_loader.py:90), [normalization patterns](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/config.py:44)

### Account reports and recurrence

**Verified:** Monthly reports aggregate signed amounts into inflows, outflows, and net for months represented in the input. Recurrence groups transactions by normalized description and direction, requires at least three occurrences, and checks weekly, biweekly, or monthly intervals. Confidence combines amount consistency, cadence consistency, and occurrence count. Fixed candidates use median amounts; variable candidates use mean amounts, annualized to monthly equivalents. [monthly aggregation](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/analysis/monthly.py:9), [defaults](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/config.py:25), [recurrence](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/analysis/recurring.py:16), [estimates](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/analysis/recurring.py:104)

The UI exposes recurring debits, variable debits, expense breakdown, recurring credits, transactions, and insights, with card details added for cards. Insights cover strongest/weakest cash-flow months, merchant concentration, spending spikes, and income variability. CSV exports include monthly summaries, transaction details, recurring tables, and card/payment details. [account UI](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/ui/app.py:205), [insights](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/analysis/insights.py:15)

### Card analysis and cross-account matching

**Verified:** Card classifiers recognize normalized `INTEREST CHARGE ON PURCHASES`, `LATE FEE`, and positive credits containing `ONLINE PAYMENT THANK YOU`. APR estimation starts an inferred balance at zero, adds charges and subtracts credits, and annualizes interest divided by the previous observed month's positive ending balance. It emits an explicit missing-statement-balance caveat and low/medium confidence. [classifiers and APR](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/analysis/credit_card.py:9)

Matching considers checking debits against recognized card payment credits. It rejects differences above $1 or beyond the configured five-day window, scores amount/date proximity and payment-like checking descriptions, and greedily selects one-to-one pairs by score. Rejected pairs are skipped; confirmation raises displayed confidence after pair selection. Match IDs contain both account storage keys and row numbers. The cross-account total includes all selected matches, including automatic ones. [matching](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/analysis/payment_matching.py:9), [scoring and IDs](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/analysis/payment_matching.py:63), [total](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/application/services.py:128)

### Feedback

**Verified:** Feedback options are automatic, confirmed, and rejected. Recurring rejection removes a candidate; recurring confirmation has no additional analytical effect. Feedback writes immediately, while application to the displayed analysis is debounced for three seconds using separate stored/applied session-state dictionaries and a one-second Streamlit fragment poll. [override application](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/application/services.py:258), [callbacks](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/ui/app.py:534), [refresh state](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/ui/app.py:633), [polling](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/ui/app.py:748)

## Gaps and limitations

1. **Verified identity design; inferred continuity limitation:** Changing CSV bytes changes the account storage key, so adding transactions, reordering rows, or changing encoding moves feedback to a new scope even for the same account. Payment identifiers additionally depend on source rows. [fingerprint](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/ingest/csv_loader.py:74), [key](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/domain/models.py:44), [match ID](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/analysis/payment_matching.py:97)

2. **Verified narrow heuristics; untested generalization:** Card labels are bank-format-specific, recurrence groups exact normalized descriptions, and monthly cadence is a 30-day approximation. Matching is greedy rather than globally optimized, and confirmation does not influence the initial ranking. APR uses no opening balance or daily statement balance; its “current” rate is the last computable rate, not necessarily the latest calendar month's rate. These limit interpretation across unfamiliar exports or incomplete histories. [card analysis](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/analysis/credit_card.py:42), [recurrence](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/analysis/recurring.py:24), [cadences](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/config.py:37), [selection](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/analysis/payment_matching.py:30)

3. **Verified aggregation scope:** Monthly aggregation includes all supplied rows, without pending-status filtering, deduplication, or transfer exclusion. Card repayments are treated as inflows in per-account summaries, and cross-account matching does not revise those summaries into a consolidated spending view. [parsing](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/ingest/csv_loader.py:111), [aggregation](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/analysis/monthly.py:9), [composition](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/application/services.py:120)

4. **Source-derived, not reproduced robustness gaps:** Short headered rows can reach direct list indexing; non-finite Decimal input has no explicit finite-value check; the UI catches only `IngestionError`. Corrupt or structurally unexpected override JSON has no recovery path, and whole-file writes can lose concurrent updates or leave partial data after interruption. [field extraction and amount parsing](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/ingest/csv_loader.py:144), [UI catch](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/ui/app.py:159), [store](/home/baquerrj/projects/simple-account-viewer/src/spending_visualizer/persistence/overrides.py:78)

Their availability and contents were deliberately not inspected. The sample selection is button-driven and not retained in session state, suggesting later reruns can drop sample inputs. Rejected candidates disappear from the lists that render their controls; there is no separate rejected-item recovery view in the inspected UI.
