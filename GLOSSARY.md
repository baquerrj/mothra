# Household Budget Tracking

The household reviews its spending against a plan so it can understand differences and decide what to adjust next month.

## Language

**Institution**:
A bank or credit union that groups the household's accounts held there, including credit-card, debit/checking, and savings accounts where applicable.

**Savings account**:
An account holding household money reserved from everyday spending until explicitly allocated or transferred for that purpose.

**Checking account**:
An account holding money used for payments, including purchases made with its linked debit cards. Multiple linked debit cards share that account's balance.

**Account nickname**:
A household-chosen name identifying an account, separate from the names of its owner or owners.

**Account owner**:
A person who owns an account. A joint account has multiple owners but represents one account and one balance.

**Household budget**:
The household's plan for spending, including essential, hobby, non-essential, and miscellaneous purchases that compete for the money available.
_Avoid_: Shared-bills-only budget

**Monthly budget review**:
A review of whether household spending stayed within its plan, why actual spending differed, and what to adjust next month.

**Monthly category budget**:
The planned amount for a spending category in a month, compared with actual spending in that category. Category amounts together form the household spending plan.

**Credit-card purchase**:
A purchase that counts toward its spending category when made and creates an obligation to repay the card.

**Credit-card repayment**:
Money moved to settle card debt, rather than a second expense for the purchases being repaid.
_Avoid_: New spending

**Actual payment source**:
The account used for a transaction, distinguishing checking/debit payments from purchases on a specific credit card.

**Budget rollover**:
Unused category spending allowance carried into a later month. It is an allowance to spend, not evidence that cash has been set aside.

**Statement balance**:
The account balance reported by an institution at a statement's closing date, representing money held or card debt owed.

**Current recorded balance**:
The account balance calculated from its dated starting balance and recorded subsequent transactions. It may differ from the institution's current balance when activity is missing or unconfirmed.

**Minimum payment due**:
The minimum payment specified on a credit-card statement for its payment due date, distinct from the statement balance and current recorded balance.

**Balance adjustment**:
An explicit, dated correction to an account balance with a recorded reason, separate from income and spending. A known missing purchase or fee is a transaction rather than a balance adjustment.
